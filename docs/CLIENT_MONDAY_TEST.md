# FaceTrack: Monday test (24 M.Tech students)

A simple plan. It takes about 30 minutes for 24 students.

## Before Monday

- **All 24 students must be ready:** each has joined the subject, registered their face, and the teacher has **approved** the registration. The system only recognises students whose face registration is approved.
- Create and open the sessions **after** those approvals. A session only includes students approved at the moment it is opened.
- Open the FaceTrack app once on the phone and once on the laptop (Chrome on a MacBook is best) and sign in, so there are no surprises on the day.
- **Close every old session** from earlier tests. Do not leave old sessions open.
- Charge the phone, keep the laptop on charge, and use a stable internet connection (Wi-Fi) on both.
- Allow camera access in the browser when asked.

## What you need

- 1 phone (Teacher app) and 1 laptop (Teacher app in Chrome).
- 24 students, and 1 volunteer to film the whole test (see the end).

## Setup (once)

1. Create **two sessions, one in each of two different classes**. Make exactly these two, and no more.
2. Open both sessions. Keep the phone on the first one and the laptop on the second one.

## Test 1: the phone (teacher walks around)

1. Press **Start scan** on the phone.
2. Take **4 students at a time**. Hold the camera steady on them for **5 seconds**, then move to the next group.
3. Record it **in one go**, from start to finish. Do not stop and restart. The app saves the video in segments by itself (20 s each); you can choose 30 s segments before you start if you like.
4. Press **Stop scan** only when everyone has been covered. Keep the app open until all segments say "sent".

## Test 2: the laptop (students come to the camera)

1. Press **Start scan** once on the laptop session.
2. Ask the students to come **one by one** in front of the laptop. Each stands **5 seconds**, then moves away.
3. Press **Stop scan** when the last student has finished. Keep the page open until uploads finish.

## Please do

- **One scan per session.** Do not create many sessions or record many videos. Earlier tests that did this overloaded the server.
- Check the screen: grey boxes mean a face is seen, amber means move closer. A student turns green in the list only after the system has recognised them.
- Look at the results and review them before publishing. Results appear on the list as each segment is processed, usually within a minute.

## Please do not

- Do not start a new session to "try again" while one is still processing.
- Do not close the app or the browser tab while segments are still uploading.

## Ask a volunteer to film everything

One volunteer should record the **whole process** on a second phone: the screen, the students and the room. If anything goes wrong, this helps us find out why quickly.

If something fails, note the **time** and the short **Reference** code shown on the error screen, and send both to us.
